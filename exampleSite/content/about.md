This is a personal knowledge base. Notes are short, dated, and filed under
exactly one category with as many tags as they need. Nothing here is finished —
a note that stops being useful gets rewritten or deleted rather than archived.

## How it is organised

Search is the primary way in. The sidebar lists categories and tags, but once a
term grows past the configured limit it stops being a useful list and becomes a
search query instead. That threshold is the one number worth tuning.

- **Categories** — one per note, describing what kind of thing it is
- **Tags** — as many as needed, describing what it is about
- **Dates** — the date the note was last meaningfully changed, not created

> The organising principle is that browsing works for tens of items and search
> works for thousands. The theme switches between them on your behalf.

## Colophon

Built with Hugo and the Ledger theme. Search is served by Pagefind, which builds
a static, chunked index at deploy time — no server, and no full index download
on first keystroke.
